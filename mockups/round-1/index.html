Mood board

Calm, dense, game-like. A control room for one person juggling many changes, not a ticket form factory.

Palette: "workbench at dusk"

Rule: amber = needs you. Purple = waiting on someone/something else. Everything else stays quiet.

Principles

Change is the pawn

One entity carries ticket, branch, worktree, PRs, environments, timeline.

"What needs me?" first

The top of every screen answers it. Waiting states are visible, not buried.

Time is visible

Age of each stage on every card. Lead time falls out of the log.

Manual first

Every transition is a button you click. Automation later replaces a click, never the model.

Freeform front-end

UI is a thin client over a boring REST API. Swap or fork views freely.

Mono for facts

Branches, SHAs, ticket keys in monospace. Prose in sans.

Reference vibes

RimWorld
